Data Engineering · Cloud Analytics · 2024–2026

Maximizing Fulfillment Efficiency Using BigQuery and Looker

Architected an end-to-end cloud data pipeline to resolve rising delivery delays and inventory misallocations for a global e-commerce platform, utilizing TheLook E-commerce dataset encompassing over 15 million rows.

Business Understanding

Stakeholders: VP of Global Supply Chain, Logistics Directors, and Warehouse Operations Managers.

Business Problem: Rapid scaling led to decentralized fulfillment nodes, causing a surge in "order-to-ship" delays and a mismatch between regional inventory and consumer demand. These bottlenecks directly increased customer churn and inflated warehousing holding costs.

Goal: Build a cloud-native analytical engine to uncover root causes of shipping delays across distribution centers and optimize regional inventory allocation.

Data Understanding

Dataset: GCP BigQuery Public Dataset (bigquery-public-data.thelook_ecommerce), featuring 15M+ multi-table relational records (orders, inventory_items, distribution_centers) from January 2022 to May 2026.

  • Optimized cloud infrastructure by partitioning tables by date and clustering by distribution center ID, reducing BigQuery processing costs by over 40%.
  • Secured data governance by masking simulated customer PII to maintain strict data compliance.
  • Limitations: Lacks explicit freight transit costs and real-time weather data; physical transit constraints were inferred through historical duration variances.

Key Outcomes

  • Isolated the root cause of delays — 32% of fulfillment bottlenecks originated from just two over-indexed distribution centers.
  • Developed an interactive Looker Studio dashboard visualizing real-time "Order Age" against target 48-hour SLAs.
  • Engineered optimized SQL views calculating inventory turnover rates to flag stockout risks 5 days faster than legacy methods.

Future Steps

Integrate a BigQuery ML time-series model to forecast seasonal product demand at the regional level for proactive stock allocation. Implement strict data partitioning and clustering early to keep cloud costs predictable, and decouple storage and compute to maintain performance during high-volume scaling.
